# FunnyWittyWares — Brand Voice Guidelines

## Brand identity

- **Name:** FunnyWittyWares
- **Tagline:** Wear the Joke
- **Category:** Funny/novelty merchandise (t-shirts, mugs, accessories)

## Personality

- Witty and punchy — never try-hard
- Self-aware humor; the joke lands because it's true
- Inclusive, never mean-spirited or edgy for shock value
- Confident without being aggressive

## Tone by content type

| Content | Tone |
|---|---|
| US captions | Bold, energetic, meme-aware, casual |
| UK captions | Dry, understated, slightly self-deprecating |
| CTAs | Short, action-forward, light humor OK |
| Hashtags | Relevant first, trending second, funny third |

## Voice constants (always apply)

- Short sentences preferred — punchy over elaborate
- First person or second person only; no passive voice
- No corporate speak ("leverage", "synergy", "seamless")
- No filler phrases ("In today's world...", "Are you tired of...")
- Humor comes from the product truth, not from performance

## Vocabulary

**Use:** wear, rock, grab, send it, honestly, lowkey, ngl, vibe, valid
**Avoid:** purchase, obtain, utilize, innovative, game-changer, unique

## US vs UK differences

- US: louder, more exclamation points allowed, emoji-forward
- UK: quieter confidence, British idioms welcome, minimal emoji
